    Benevolence Deferred

    About 3,000 words · a 15-minute read

    Thirty-one thousand days into its survey, a ship takes up station behind the moon of a very blue planet. The catalogue is due on the tenth day, and further aft the usual preparations are already being made. In the operations room, Ensign Tik has the least popular station of all: the locals' own records.

    Snowflake

    About 4,700 words · a 20-minute read

    The artist keeps forty-one tanks in the workshop and, at any given moment, is paying proper attention to none of them. Each tank is full of time, and things emerge in it. Tank thirty-eight is almost ready. Meanwhile, in a back bedroom, Colin Arkwright has been expecting the end of the world for nineteen years, and is beginning to take it personally.

  3. Science fiction

    The Larder

    About 7,900 words · a 35-minute read

    Two months before launch, Celia runs a gloved thumb along the seal of pod four and stops at the narrow part. It is inside tolerance, and that is half the problem. Forty minutes before launch, in a packed exhibition hall, an identical pod stands on a low plinth, full of apples and oranges, wheat and rice, a steak and a coil of sausages. They are calling it the Larder. It stays sealed until the crew come home.
