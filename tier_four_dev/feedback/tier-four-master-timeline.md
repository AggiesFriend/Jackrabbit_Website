# Coming of Age – proposed master timeline

Companion to *tier-four-canon-check.md*. This is a **proposal for Tony to approve**. Once agreed, it should become the reference for the manuscript, the Book 4 plan and the *Tier Four* game, and be copied into the series tracker.

## 1. How the anchors were chosen

The three sources disagree:

| Milestone | Manuscript (Ch 1–18) | Book 4 plan | Game script |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 2 | Under a year | 18 months (Jack 15) | 8 months |
| Tier 3 | 2 years 3 months | Jack 17 | About 2 years 8 months, Jack 16 |
| Tier 4 | "Almost four years" | Jack 19 | 6 years, Jack 19 |
| Secret tier | – | Tier 5, Jack 20 | (Tier 4) |
| Book 5 opens | – | Jack 21 (Book 5 arc) | – |

Jack is 13 at Prometheus in all three, and 21 at the start of Book 5. That leaves about eight years to fill. The manuscript's own durations only cover about four of them, and its stated ages (16 shortly after Tier 2) don't fit its durations.

**Recommendation:** keep the **plan's and game's ages** (they protect Book 5) and keep the **manuscript's "Tier 2 in under a year" record** (Ch 6 builds a whole scene on it being unprecedented). The long Tier 2 stretch then absorbs the difference. This means changing some durations in the manuscript, but none of its scenes or events.

*Alternative (not recommended):* keep the manuscript's durations. Jack would then be about 17 at Tier 4, and Book 5 would open with him at about 18 or 19, so the Book 5 arc's "Jack (21)" would need changing.
